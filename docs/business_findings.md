## Finding 1: Revenue growth is expansion-led

**Evidence**

Across the modeled window:

- Expansion MRR: **$8.63M**
- New MRR: **$2.55M**
- Average NRR: **119%** (range 111.7%–151.8%)

In December 2024 alone, expansion (`$1.70M`) exceeded new MRR (`$521.8K`).
